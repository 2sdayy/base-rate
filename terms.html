What this is

Base Rate reads publicly available on-chain trade history and scores it - matching buys to sells, accounting for fees, and reporting the result. It is a research and data product. It is not investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and not a signal or copy-trading service.

No guarantee of accuracy

Data is derived by sampling public blockchain activity and is subject to the methodology's known limits - wallets are found by sampling activity, so any given snapshot can be incomplete, and past performance describes what already happened, not what happens next. We work to keep the methodology honest and documented, but make no guarantee the data is complete, current, or error-free.
